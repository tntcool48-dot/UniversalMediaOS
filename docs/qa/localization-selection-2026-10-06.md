# Selected labels and Arabic Downloads — October 6, 2026

Changing the isolated app from English to Arabic saved `Arabic`, while its language selector visibly retained the English label. Changing back could retain Arabic. Downloads also displayed English queue actions/states and visually reversed size/progress strings such as `MB 212.6` and `%4`.

## Repair

The runtime localization walker wrote a local `Text` value into WPF's implicit string presenter. That presenter is owned by its `ContentPresenter`; it does not expose a normal data binding, but assigning its text prevents future selected-content updates. The walker now leaves those implicit presenters to WPF and preserves all binding kinds, including multi-bindings and bound tooltips. Reused unbound labels also discard a remembered original when the element receives new source text.

Downloads supplies bound state/message text through a small localization multi-binding that refreshes on source and language changes. The dictionary now includes queue actions/states and the demonstrated paused/completed messages. Unknown provider/error text passes through unchanged. Filename, size and progress text keep their left-to-right order within the Arabic page; the surrounding layout remains right-to-left. This changes neither the queue's saved states nor download ownership.
