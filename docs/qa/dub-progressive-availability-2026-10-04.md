# Progressive Dub availability — October 4, 2026

Dub-filtered searches previously withheld a whole catalog page until every title's availability lookup finished. Details also withheld an exact-card badge while checking episode flags. Cancel only covered a search launched through SearchCommand, so automatic filter refreshes and paging could continue without a working visible Cancel action.

The availability service now publishes ordered snapshots and separate completed, pending, timeout, unavailable, backoff and identity-conflict outcomes. Up to two configured availability providers run concurrently per lookup; existing title concurrency, host spacing, response-size, body-deadline and discovery limits remain. Exact-card summaries can appear before episode verification. Fast positive results survive another provider's failure, verified episode flags outrank badge summaries, and a pending provider prevents an interim zero from declaring verified absence. A known MAL conflict withdraws that provider's provisional summary. Matching, unit and audio-flag parsing were not weakened.

Search publishes the catalog page promptly, retains pending/unknown titles, and removes completed checked-zero titles as before. Visible-card annotations and Details also consume progressive results. Queued or old-generation updates cannot replace terminal/current results. Cancel covers manual searches, recommendations, automatic filters, paging and Details, retains useful results and cancels only the caller's share of a lookup. A remaining subscriber keeps its request alive; cancellation of the final owner releases network/body work without marking a provider offline. Retrying a canceled partial page replaces its exact AniList identities instead of duplicating cards. Incomplete results use the short unknown-cache lifetime rather than a long positive lifetime.

These availability dependencies remain separate from anime playback discovery through EverythingMoe/The Index. No playback provider picker or seed list was added. Badge counts and per-episode flags describe provider evidence; they do not establish spoken language.
