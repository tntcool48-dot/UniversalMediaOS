## How to use the checkboxes

- A checked **implementation step** means only that named change and its recorded tests/probes were completed. It does not mean its entire feature works end to end.
- Leave **package acceptance** and **release acceptance** unchecked until their stated behavior is demonstrated. Record the build, test/profile, observed result and remaining limitation alongside the item or in linked QA notes. Earlier dated observations are not current provider-health claims.
- Work on one concrete user problem per batch. Add a helper, abstraction, migration or test only when needed to fix that problem or prevent a demonstrated regression. Do not expand the plan merely to complete more internal checkboxes.
- After each batch, update this file with what changed, what was actually verified and the next action. Preserve original requirements; explicitly explain any scope change. Do not report a completion percentage based on the number of internal tasks checked.
