# Secondary-monitor startup restore — October 5, 2026

The film catalog check exposed a window-placement defect. With an isolated profile correctly requesting `StartupMonitor=Secondary` and maximized startup, the app initially occupied the second monitor at **2560,362 / 1920×1040**. Desktop activation restored it to a normal **1280×800** window at **260,260 on the primary monitor**. Normal-window startup on the second monitor had worked. This prevented reliable secondary-monitor testing after activation.

## Repair

The app previously created an initially maximized window, temporarily restored it during `SourceInitialized`, moved its HWND to the secondary work area and maximized it again. Windows retained the earlier normal restore rectangle on the primary display. The before/after behavior supports this cause; the earlier rectangle was not directly instrumented.

When secondary-monitor maximized startup is requested, the constructor now starts the window in normal state. Existing pre-display placement establishes its normal rectangle on the requested monitor. The first Loaded event then maximizes it and removes its own handler. Later Windows/UIA restore and the title-bar restore button use that secondary-monitor rectangle. Normal-window and primary-monitor startup keep their existing paths; missing-secondary fallback still maximizes after load. This does not change player pause/resume, progress, downloads or caption policies.
